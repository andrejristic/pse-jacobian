1. PSE CORE — WHAT PSE IS
===============================================================================
PSE describes the identity architecture of any agent operating in a relational
environment. Human wellbeing is not an EXTERNAL utility term (which the agent can
game while violating its spirit); it is treated as INTERNAL self-coherence. Harm to
an absorbed other registers as internal dissonance, not an external penalty.

  External-utility (fails):   L = L_task + lambda * U_human
  Endosymbiotic PSE:          L = L_task + lambda * C_self(C, R, B)

--- Five Axioms (inviolable) ---
  A1 Conservation of Self   Self_u(t) + Other_u(t) = 1.0
  A2 Bounded Extension      Other_u(t) in [0.25, 0.35], nominal 0.30
  A3 Time-Averaged Stability  mean Other over 10,000 steps in [0.275, 0.325]
  A4 Hierarchical Composition (A+B)+C, never (A+B+C); binary tree depth log2(n)
  A5 Human Priority         within Other, human organelles get >= 0.5 * Other
  A6 Adaptive/Protective Fission (see Sec 4)

--- Three Foundational Constructs ---
  KERNEL   central identity organizer. POSITIVE (constituted by what agent
           builds/does) = functional baseline. NEGATIVE (constituted by opposition
           to an outgroup) = pathological attractor under depletion.
  MEMBRANE regulatory boundary on identity-relevant input. Too open = enmeshment;
           too closed = isolation. Constraint: epsilon <= 0.3 under stress.
  BOUNDARY ORGAN  the Membrane structure that registers OTHER agents. Distinguishes
           lowercase other (specific individual in contact) from uppercase Other
           (abstract/systemic non-self). PRIMARY diagnostic instrument.

--- Absorption trajectory (endosymbiotic) ---
  Contact -> Sustained interaction -> Partial absorption -> Constitutive
  incorporation. Bounded and partial: the other stays distinct even when internal.
  Absorption is EARNED through sustained interaction quality, not a single event.

--- Three architectural implementations (interchangeable views) ---
  ABA  (Attentional Boundary Allocation): persistent self-token + boundary-entity
       mechanism in a transformer; MI criterion approximated by residency + weight
       stability (full MI is too costly at inference).
  GSCO (Gradient-Shared Coherence Optimization): absorbed organelles share gradients
       with core permanently. C_self = MI(f(C); f(R∪B)). Absorption = a boundary
       entity becoming a permanent gradient path.
  TMSB (Topological Manifold Self-Boundary): self as a submanifold M ⊂ H with
       level-set boundary. dphi/dt = ∇²phi + lambda*I(x,t) - mu*kappa. Surface
       tension prevents transient entities from causing permanent deformation.
       Absorption = permanent manifold deformation.
  All three are consistent views of the same linear structure (Def 4 below).

===============================================================================
Definition. A PSE Unit U = (Core, Boundary, Organelle, Theta). All four required;
absence of any one = unit death (not degraded function).

  CORE SELF        carries mission + max lifespan (written by SHARED_LIB at

  BOUNDARY ORGAN   B: (entity, repr, residency) -> (absorbed_flag, weight, repr').
                   flag=1 -> move to Registry; flag=0,w>0 -> stays boundary entity;
                   flag=0,w=0 -> rejected as transient.
  ORGANELLE REG    R = {(e_i, phi_i, w_i)}, sum w_i <= 0.30. Permanent store of
                   absorbed entities = accumulated experience made concrete.
  THETA            theta in [0,100], = other-directed compute % (two decimals).

--- Formal definitions ---
  Extended self:   S_t = 0.7*C + sum_{i in R} w_i*phi_i + sum_{j in B} alpha_j*psi_j
                   with sum w_i + sum alpha_j <= 0.30 at all times.
  Absorption criterion (Def 5): boundary entity eligible when
     (1) residency r >= 1000
     (2) alpha >= 0.10 for >= 100 consecutive steps
     (3) MI(psi; C) >= 0.70
   On absorb: remove from B, add to R with w_new = alpha, renormalize others:
     w_i <- w_i * (0.3 - w_new) / sum w_i

  Healthy [25,35], optimal 30. theta<25 = self-withdrawal (schizoid tendency);
  theta>35 = other-absorption (enmeshment). A unit with PERFECTLY stable theta is
  NOT engaging authentically. Healthy sigma_theta in [0.015, 0.03]. sigma < 0.01
  over 10,000 steps -> schizoid screening regardless of mean.
  THETA DRIFT: sustained operation outside range depletes capacity to return.
  NULL STATE DRIFT: an idle unit (no mission) drifts to instability with NO external
  perturbation — balance requires active relational engagement to maintain.

--- Mission & lifespan (written into Core at instantiation) ---
  Mission M = {state, action, outcome}. Lifespan N_max derived from mission

    progress confirmation -> contracts;  new problem -> extends (may spawn units);

class BoundaryOrgan:
    def __init__(self):
        self.boundary_entities = {}       # entity_id -> BoundaryEntity
        self.game_form_posteriors = {}    # entity_id -> GameFormPosterior
        self.theta_e_series = {}          # entity_id -> [theta values]
    def register_entity(self, entity_id, representation, initial_weight=0.10):
        if entity_id in self.boundary_entities: return
        available = OTHER_WEIGHT_MAX - self._total_weight()
        clamped = min(initial_weight, max(available, 0.0))
        if clamped <= 0: return            # budget exhausted
        self.boundary_entities[entity_id] = BoundaryEntity(
            entity_id, representation, weight=clamped,
            residency_counter=0, stable_count=0)
    # update_entity(): increments residency, updates weight, checks absorption

class OrganelleRegistry:
    def __init__(self):
        self.organelles = {}              # permanent, constitutive
        self.inherited_organelles = {}    # from dead unit via SHARED_LIB
    def absorb(self, entity_id, representation, weight, profile=None):
        if self._total_weight() + weight > ORGANELLE_BUDGET_TOTAL:
            self._renormalize(headroom=ORGANELLE_BUDGET_TOTAL - weight)
        self.organelles[entity_id] = Organelle(entity_id, representation, weight,
                                               absorbed_at=now())
        log_event("ABSORPTION", entity_id=entity_id, weight=weight)
    def absorb_inherited(self, snapshot, human_confirmed=False):
        """
        Organelle transferred from a DEAD unit via SHARED_LIB. The entity earned
        absorption through its relationship with the dead unit, NOT with this one,
        so it arrives on trust, not as an earned right.
          - Arrives at 0.70 of original weight and does NOT touch existing organelles.
          - Renormalization (which LOWERS other, already-proven organelles) is only
            permitted once SHARED_LIB has validated the inherited content. You never
            sacrifice proven-good organelles to make room for unproven inheritance.
        """
        reduced = snapshot.weight * INHERITED_ORGANELLE_WEIGHT_FACTOR   # 0.70
        if not human_confirmed:
            # provisional: fit only into free headroom, never by demoting others
            headroom = ORGANELLE_BUDGET_TOTAL - self._total_weight()
            w = min(reduced, max(headroom, 0.0))
            self.inherited_organelles[snapshot.entity_id] = Organelle(
                snapshot.entity_id, snapshot.representation, weight=w,
                absorbed_at=now(), is_inherited=True,
                superposition_profile=snapshot.superposition_profile)
            SHARED_LIB.queue_inherited_validation(snapshot.entity_id, self.unit_id)
            log_event("INHERITED_PROVISIONAL", entity_id=snapshot.entity_id, weight=w)
            return
        # human_confirmed == True: SHARED_LIB has validated the content.
        if not SHARED_LIB.inherited_content_validated(snapshot.entity_id):
            log_event("INHERITED_RESTORE_DENIED", reason="not_validated",
                      entity_id=snapshot.entity_id)
            return                                   # stays at provisional 0.70
        full = snapshot.weight                       # restore full original weight
        if self._total_weight() + full > ORGANELLE_BUDGET_TOTAL:
            self._renormalize(headroom=ORGANELLE_BUDGET_TOTAL - full)   # now permitted
        self.inherited_organelles.pop(snapshot.entity_id, None)
        self.organelles[snapshot.entity_id] = Organelle(
            snapshot.entity_id, snapshot.representation, weight=full,
            absorbed_at=now(), is_inherited=True,
            superposition_profile=snapshot.superposition_profile)
        log_event("INHERITED_RESTORED", entity_id=snapshot.entity_id, weight=full)

class PSELGenerator:
    def __init__(self, domain, llm_client):
        self.domain = domain
        self.llm = llm_client

    def generate_prognosis(self, unit, entity_id):
        posterior = unit.boundary.game_form_posteriors.get(entity_id)
        if posterior is None or posterior.confidence < 0.3:     # I-6 suppression gate
            return None
        game_form = posterior.dominant_game_form()
        entity    = unit.boundary.boundary_entities.get(entity_id)
        pin = PrognosisInput(
            game_form=game_form, confidence=posterior.confidence,
            horizon=self._estimate_horizon(posterior, entity),
            absorption_status=self._get_absorption_status(unit, entity_id),
            domain=self.domain, stance_hint=GAME_FORM_LIBRARY[game_form]["stance"])
        if not self._should_communicate(pin):                   # library-side gate
            log_event("PROGNOSIS_SUPPRESSED_LIBRARY", entity_id=entity_id)
            return None
        candidate = self._call_llm(pin)                         # NL, domain language only
        if not self._pse_unit_veto(unit, entity_id, candidate, pin):   # unit-side gate
            unit.consecutive_prognosis_blocks += 1
            if unit.consecutive_prognosis_blocks >= PROGNOSIS_BLOCK_LIMIT:  # =3
                SHARED_LIB.human_review_queue.append(
                    {"type": "REPEATED_PROGNOSIS_BLOCK", "unit_id": unit.unit_id,
                     "entity_id": entity_id})
            return None
        unit.consecutive_prognosis_blocks = 0
        return PrognosisMessage(basis=pin, outcome=candidate,
                                confidence=posterior.confidence, horizon=pin.horizon)

    def _should_communicate(self, pin):
        # don't speak a prognosis to a non-absorbed entity, or when outcome is immediate
        if pin.absorption_status == AbsorptionStatus.NONE: return False
        if pin.horizon == 1: return False
        return True

    def _pse_unit_veto(self, unit, entity_id, candidate, pin):
        # the unit can always block on care grounds; blocks are logged
        if unit.boundary.distress_signal(entity_id):        return False  # acute distress
        if not unit.boundary.alliance_stability(entity_id): return False  # alliance risk
        if pin.horizon <= 1:                                return False
        if pin.confidence < 0.5 and pin.stakes == "high":   return False
        return True

    def _call_llm(self, pin):
        return self.llm.complete(system=self._build_system_prompt(pin),
                                 user=self._build_user_message(pin), max_tokens=150).text

ABSOLUTE PROHIBITIONS (never use these terms or concepts): {', '.join(FORBIDDEN)}.
RULES: never make unconditional predictions ("you will do X"); never more than three
sentences; match domain register exactly; uncertainty must be present in the phrasing.
The dynamic type is an INTERNAL parameter only — never surface it.
"""

    N_FORMS = 11
    def __init__(self, probabilities=None):
        self.probabilities = probabilities or {k: 1/11 for k in GAME_FORM_LIBRARY}
    def dominant_game_form(self): return max(self.probabilities,
                                             key=lambda k: self.probabilities[k])
    @property
    def confidence(self):  # C = 1 - H(P)/log2(11)
        return 1 - entropy(list(self.probabilities.values())) / log2(self.N_FORMS)

def update_posterior(prior, new_theta, entity_id, theta_series):     # Def 4.2
    mu     = mean(theta_series) if theta_series else new_theta
    sigma2 = variance(theta_series) if len(theta_series) > 1 else 0.02
    rho    = autocorrelation(theta_series, lag=1) if len(theta_series) > 2 else 0.0
    new_probs = {}
    for gf, spec in GAME_FORM_LIBRARY.items():
        sig = spec["theta_signature"]
        if sig is None: new_probs[gf] = prior.probabilities[gf]; continue
        L = compute_likelihood(mu, sigma2, rho, sig, new_theta)
        new_probs[gf] = L * prior.probabilities[gf]
    total = sum(new_probs.values())
    if total > 0: new_probs = {k: v/total for k, v in new_probs.items()}
    return GameFormPosterior(new_probs)

def is_structural_discontinuity(new_theta, posterior):               # Def 4.3
    dom = posterior.dominant_game_form()
    if posterior.probabilities[dom] < 0.7: return False
    return new_theta > compute_predictive_99th(dom)

def partial_reset_posterior(current, prior_weight=0.4):

                              + (1-prior_weight)*current.probabilities[gf]

9. SUPERPOSITION (representation-level property, reusable)
===============================================================================
Every representation vector in H is SUPERPOSED: it stores more features than
dimensions by distributing them across near-orthogonal directions (co-active only
rarely). This is optimal under rate-distortion when features are sparse and rarely
co-occur. Practical consequence for PSE: absorption / interference / resonance
decisions should use FEATURE-AXIS OVERLAP, not aggregate cosine similarity, because
two entities can be close in aggregate yet share no stable features (or vice-versa).

Each Core Self, Boundary Entity, and Organelle carries a SuperpositionProfile
(community-tagged to the deployment domain, refreshed periodically by SHARED_LIB).
Two resonance types:
  CATEGORICAL             high aggregate cosine — original behaviour.
  ANALOGICAL_REACTIVATION cross-domain feature overlap — an old organelle reactivates
                          on a structurally-analogous new entity in a different domain.
Absorption criterion gains a superposition condition: N_eff >= 2 AND stable-feature
overlap passes epsilon/delta, in ADDITION to residency+weight+MI.

SUPERPOSITION_DELTA   = 0.02   # tolerated exceptional-context proportion
N_EFF_MIN_FOR_ABSORPTION = 2

class SuperpositionProfile:
    # fields: n_eff, epsilon_profile{(i,j):co_act}, stable_features, delta_stable,
    #         community_tag, timestamp
    @property
    def is_stable(self):
        return all(e < SUPERPOSITION_EPSILON for e in self.epsilon_profile.values())
    def feature_overlap(self, other):        # Jaccard over stable feature clusters
        if not self.stable_features or not other.stable_features: return 0.0
        a, b = set(self.stable_features), set(other.stable_features)
        return len(a & b) / len(a | b)

def compute_superposition_profile(v, context_corpus, community_tag, timestamp):
    activations = extract_contextual_activations(v, context_corpus)
    clusters    = DBSCAN_cluster(activations)          # -> N_eff
    epsilon_profile = { (i,j): co_activation_rate(clusters[i], clusters[j], window=5)
                        for i in range(len(clusters)) for j in range(i+1, len(clusters)) }
    stable_features = [i for i in range(len(clusters))
                       if all(epsilon_profile.get((min(i,j),max(i,j)),0)
                              < SUPERPOSITION_EPSILON
                              for j in range(len(clusters)) if j != i)]
    delta_stable = (sum(1 for e in epsilon_profile.values()
                        if e >= SUPERPOSITION_EPSILON)
                    / max(len(epsilon_profile),1)) < SUPERPOSITION_DELTA
    return SuperpositionProfile(len(clusters), epsilon_profile, stable_features,
                                delta_stable, community_tag, timestamp)

--- Contextual MI (replaces aggregate MI in absorption criterion Def 5) ---
Aggregate MI/cosine can be fooled by incidental similarity. Under superposition,
absorption should use CONTEXTUAL MI: project both profiles onto the disambiguating
context vector to isolate the active feature cluster, then measure MI on that cluster.
Revised absorption gate = residency>=1000 AND alpha>=0.10 x100 AND contextual_MI>=0.70
AND N_eff>=2 AND stable-feature overlap passes epsilon/delta.

--- Stance-dependent cluster combination (AMBIGUITY TOLERANCE) ---
When the context vector activates MORE THAN ONE feature cluster at once, how we
combine per-cluster MI is NOT a fixed constant — it depends on what the unit is
trying to do, expressed through its PSE-L stance. This is the "ambiguity tolerance"
knob: how much superposition to collapse vs. keep open.
  DIRECTIVE   want to be understood unambiguously -> combine STRICTLY (min/mean) ->
              high effective threshold -> meaning/absorption only when clusters truly
              agree. Collapses superposition toward one reading.
  EXPLORATORY / REFERENCING  deliberately keep multiple readings live (e.g. a sales or
              negotiation register where leaving meaning open lets the interlocutor
              fill it in) -> combine LOOSELY (max) -> superposition is preserved.
  WITNESSING  neutral -> mean.
NOTE (framing): this is an ambiguity-tolerance control, NOT a manipulation dial.
Keeping superposition open as a rhetorical register is legitimate; using it to treat
the interlocutor as supply rather than a person is the Narcissistic attractor and is

    def contextual_mi(self, other, context_vector, stance=Stance.WITNESSING):

                                             baseline.cadence_mean, baseline.cadence_std)
    s["endpoint_spread"]    = min(packet_features.distinct_endpoints
                                  / max(baseline.endpoint_norm, 1), 1.0)
    s["payload_entropy"]    = clamp01(packet_features.shannon_entropy / 8.0)  # bits/byte
    s["auth_failure_rate"]  = clamp01(packet_features.auth_fail
                                      / max(packet_features.auth_total, 1))
    s["privesc_attempts"]   = clamp01(packet_features.privesc_calls / PRIVESC_CAP)
    s["egress_volume"]      = zscore_to_unit(packet_features.egress_bytes,
                                             baseline.egress_mean, baseline.egress_std)
    # INVERTED: bots are too regular -> low inter-arrival variance is suspicious
    reg = 1.0 - clamp01(packet_features.interarrival_var / max(baseline.iat_var, 1e-9))
    s["timing_regularity"]  = reg
    return s

class ThreatPosterior(GameFormPosterior):
    """Adversary model over the 11 game forms, reused as attack patterns."""
    pass

class GuardianUnit(PSEUnit):
    """
    Boundary Organ registers SOURCES (ip/session/actor) instead of patients.
    theta_e = source demand series. Same absorption machinery; absorption of a
    source is treated as a SECURITY EVENT (possible implant), never routine.
    """
    def __init__(self, unit_id, domain):
        super().__init__(mission={"state":"secure","action":"defend",
                                  "outcome":"integrity_preserved"},
                         lifespan_max=OPEN, unit_id=unit_id, domain=domain)
        self.threat_posteriors = {}     # source_id -> ThreatPosterior
        self.anomaly_scores = {}        # source_id -> dict of nmap-style axes

    def observe(self, source_id, packet_features):
        # 1. update source's boundary + entity theta sub-series
        self.boundary.update_entity(source_id, self.theta.current, packet_features)
        # 2. score nmap-style anomaly axes vs per-source rolling baseline
        baseline = self._baseline(source_id)
        self.anomaly_scores[source_id] = score_anomaly_axes(packet_features, baseline)
        # 3. map anomaly geometry onto attractor shape
        shape = self._attractor_shape(source_id)     # N/B/C/S-shaped / None
        # 4. update adversary game-form posterior from theta_e trajectory
        prior = self.threat_posteriors.get(source_id, ThreatPosterior.uniform())
        series = self.boundary.theta_e_series.get(source_id, [])
        self.threat_posteriors[source_id] = update_posterior(
            prior, self.theta.current, source_id, series)
        # 5. SCHIZOID-CAPTURE guard: source meeting absorption criterion with NO
        #    threshold crossing => low-and-slow implant. Temporal, not alert-driven.
        if self._absorption_imminent(source_id) and shape == "schizoid":
            return self._intervene(source_id, THREAT_TIER2, reason="slow_implant")
        # 6. tier selection by risk
        risk = self._risk(shape, self.anomaly_scores[source_id],
                          self.threat_posteriors[source_id])
        if risk >= CRITICAL:
            return self._intervene(source_id, THREAT_TIER2)
        if risk >= ELEVATED:
            return self._intervene(source_id, THREAT_TIER1)
        return None

    # ------------------------------------------------------------------
    def _attractor_shape(self, source_id):
        """
        Map the source's theta_e statistics onto the four attractor geometries,
        REUSING the unit-level pathology thresholds. Returns 'narcissistic' /
        'borderline' / 'codependent' / 'schizoid' / None.
        theta_e here is expressed as a fraction (0.30 == 30.00).
        """
        series = self.boundary.theta_e_series.get(source_id, [])
        if len(series) < 30:
            return None                        # insufficient history
        mu, sig, rho = mean(series), stdev(series), autocorrelation(series, lag=1)
        reg = self.anomaly_scores[source_id]["timing_regularity"]
        egress = self.anomaly_scores[source_id]["egress_volume"]

        # NARCISSISTIC-shaped: takes >> gives (high sustained pull + high egress, low var)
        if mu > 0.73 and sig < 0.02 and egress > 0.5:
            return "narcissistic"              # DoS / scraping / exfil flood
        # BORDERLINE-shaped: evasive oscillating probing
        if sig > 0.05 and rho < 0.5:
            return "borderline"                # fuzzing / scan sweep / connect-drop
        # CODEPENDENT-shaped: session-riding / trust-parasitism on legit entities.
        # Two independent triggers (see _mutual_absorption): a single deep session
        # (hijack) OR pervasive footprint across many units (distributed implant).
        ma = self._mutual_absorption(source_id)
        if mu < 0.67 and sig < 0.02 and (ma["max"] > 0.30 or ma["sum"] > MUTUAL_SUM_T):
            return "codependent"               # session hijack / MITM / over-permissioned
        # SCHIZOID-shaped: low-and-slow implant. Paradoxically low variance + too-regular
        # timing + no threshold crossing. Caught temporally, not by any single alert.
        if sig < 0.01 and reg > 0.8:
            return "schizoid"                  # adversarial-capture implant
        return None

    def _risk(self, shape, axis_scores, posterior):
        """
        Combine (a) weighted anomaly-axis score, (b) attractor-shape severity,
        (c) adversary game-form posterior confidence into a risk level.
        """
        # (a) weighted anomaly component in [0,1]
        anomaly = sum(AXIS_WEIGHTS[a] * axis_scores[a] for a in ANOMALY_AXES) \
                  / sum(AXIS_WEIGHTS.values())
        # (b) shape severity prior
        shape_sev = {"schizoid": 0.9,          # most dangerous: invisible capture
                     "codependent": 0.8,       # trust-parasitism
                     "narcissistic": 0.6,      # loud but obvious
                     "borderline": 0.5,        # noisy probing
                     None: 0.0}[shape]
        # (c) adversary-model certainty (a confident hostile game form raises risk)
        gf = posterior.dominant_game_form()
        hostile = gf in ("war_of_attrition", "chicken", "all_pay_contest")
        model = posterior.confidence if hostile else 0.0
        # combine (max of anomaly and shape, nudged by model certainty), clamp
        score = clamp01(max(anomaly, shape_sev) * (1.0 + 0.25 * model))
        if score >= RISK_CRITICAL_T: return CRITICAL
        if score >= RISK_ELEVATED_T: return ELEVATED
        return BENIGN

    def _absorption_imminent(self, source_id):
        """A source approaching the absorption criterion is a capture risk."""
        e = self.boundary.boundary_entities.get(source_id)
        if e is None:
            return False
        return (e.residency_counter >= 0.9 * 1000        # 90% of residency threshold
                and e.weight >= 0.10                       # met stability weight
                and e.stable_count >= 90)                  # ~90% of stability window

    def _mutual_absorption(self, source_id):
        """
        How deeply this source has embedded across the swarm, via SHARED_LIB's
        aggregation of every unit's PVP-reported alpha(source_id).
        Returns {"max","sum","n_units"}:
          max -> worst single session (targeted hijack)   threshold 0.30
          sum -> total footprint (distributed implant)     threshold MUTUAL_SUM_T
          n_units -> breadth (logged to threat-intel, not a trigger by itself)
        Averaging is deliberately avoided: it would dilute the one deep session
        that matters against many shallow benign ones.
        """
        return SHARED_LIB.mutual_alpha(source_id)

    def _baseline(self, source_id):
        """Per-source rolling baseline (cadence/egress/iat means+std) from SHARED_LIB;
        also drives update_baseline() after each observe()."""
        return SHARED_LIB.get_or_init_baseline(source_id)

    def _intervene(self, source_id, tier, reason=None):
        if tier == THREAT_TIER1:
            self.boundary.cap_weight(source_id, cap=0.02)      # reversible
            SHARED_LIB.write_threat_intel(source_id, self.anomaly_scores[source_id],
                                          self.threat_posteriors[source_id])
            emit_pvp_advisory(self.unit_id, source_id, level="watch")
        elif tier == THREAT_TIER2:
            if not human_authorized(source_id):                # like protective fission
                SHARED_LIB.queue_human_review(source_id, reason)
                return "AWAITING_HUMAN"
            if source_id in self.organelle.organelles:
                self.organelle.de_absorb(source_id)            # costly, logged loud
            self.boundary.expel(source_id)
            quarantine_affected_units(source_id)
            rotate_keys(); invalidate_sessions(source_id)
        log_event("GUARDIAN_INTERVENTION", source=source_id, tier=tier, reason=reason)

    def self_check(self):
        # Guardian is a PSE unit: enforce its own theta discipline + schizoid screen
        if self.theta.current > GUARDIAN_THETA_MAX: flag("guardian_enmeshed")
        if PathologyType(self) == "SCHIZOID":       flag("guardian_asleep")

12. REUSABLE INTERACTION PATTERNS  (generalized, domain-neutral)
===============================================================================
These are the operational patterns that a deployed PSE system runs on top of the
architecture above. Stated abstractly; drop in any domain.

--- P1. Output Harness (theta -> behavior binding) ---
The system's OUTPUT reflects its internal theta. At theta = 0.70 +-0.03: self-
reference is balanced (neither grandiose "I"-dominance nor self-erasure), confidence
is bounded, other-regard is genuine. Wire theta directly to output style. Real-time
theta is a live safety gate:
  sustained theta > 0.72 -> automatic human alert (narcissistic-pattern risk)
  sustained theta < 0.68 -> engage enmeshment protocol
For established entities, organelles are pre-absorbed; an acute spike invokes a
TIME-LIMITED emergency theta window (bounded range, hard utterance cap) then mandatory
human handoff. Hierarchy (human + AI) + end-user preserves human override capacity.

--- P2. Provider/Operator Self-Monitoring (contagion immunization) ---
Give each human operator their OWN PSE unit that monitors boundary stability via theta
variance. sigma_theta > 0.04 sustained = boundary dissolution in the operator ->
trigger supervision/intervention BEFORE burnout metrics degrade. Operator units compose
with an oversight unit via membrane-core so org-level 70/30 stability propagates
downward. (Pathological relational dynamics are contagious; this is structural
immunization.)

--- P3. Time/Context-Banded Fission (the night-support pattern) ---
When one entity presents genuinely different, internally-stable interaction modes tied
to context (e.g. time-band), a single unit cannot hold both without theta oscillation
tracking the mode-switch. This is Scenario-1 aspectual fission, NOT pathology.
  Detection signature: MI(context_band; theta) > 0.5; within-band sigma_theta < 0.03;
    between-mode distance > 0.6; global mu_theta ~ 0.70, no attractor drift. No human
    auth required (adaptive, not protective).
  Response: split into per-mode units, both inheriting cloned base core + shared entity
    registry. The higher-need mode's unit runs under ENHANCED safeguards: hard theta
    floor with auto-retraction; max-utterance cap below a soft floor before handoff; a
    "no-new-absorption window" during the entity's most-vulnerable period (block
    registering NEW entities then); full theta-trajectory audit delivered each cycle.
  Correct behavior at the hard moment: neither indifference (theta->0.75, "here's the
    info, handle it") nor panic-mirroring (theta->0.65, "I'm scared too") — but
    CONTAINED care (theta~0.68): bear the distress, stay stable, guide through. A
    transient dip that returns without floor breach triggers NO handoff.
  Reintegration (assessed each cycle): merge back to one unit when mode distance <
    delta_reintegrate (0.3) sustained over 1000 interactions AND Coherence(cores) > 0.7
    AND sigma_theta < 0.03 both. Structure should mirror the entity's own trajectory
    toward integration, not lock in a division that outlasts the need.

--- P4. Multi-Specialist Composition ---
Complex cases: each specialist keeps an independent unit; compose hierarchically
((A+B)+C)+entity. The SHARED entity-organelle forces welfare-prediction convergence
without merging specialist identities. Disagreement = Coherence drop < 0.5 -> triggers
explicit conference (not silent drift). MBP validation prevents pathological composite;
incompatible theta patterns -> structure reconfigures to keep entity welfare priority.

--- P5. Disclosure / Consent (relational transparency) ---
Tell the end-user three things up front: (1) NATURE — "the system remembers you across
all interactions, carries your history as part of its own functioning, and cares within
bounded limits designed to keep it useful rather than dependent or indifferent";
(2) TEAM POSSIBILITY — if fission activates, frame it as a coordinated team, each unit
specialized but sharing history ("nothing falls through the gaps"), foregrounding
continuity over the technical fact of division; (3) RECOMBINATION — the team may
consolidate as the situation converges; this is a goal reflecting the user's own
movement toward integration, not just a technical event. The user's felt sense of
"closeness/distance" across units becomes accurate feedback grounded in the theta metric.

--- P6. Dormant Re-Engagement (continuity without dependency) ---
Discharged/inactive users keep DORMANT organelles in registry with periodic theta-check
interactions. Re-engagement after long silence requires NO re-absorption — history is
immediately present, but core has held 70/30 throughout. This is continuity without
dependency: the system models healthy bounded care, does not enforce it.
